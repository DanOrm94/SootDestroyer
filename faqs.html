How often should I have my chimney swept?+

Annual sweeping is a sensible guide for many appliances. Frequency can vary depending on what you burn and how often the appliance is used.

Do you sweep log burners?+

Yes. We can sweep suitable log burners, multi-fuel stoves and open fires.

Will you leave the room clean?+

Clean, careful workmanship is part of the service. We take reasonable precautions to protect the surrounding area.

Can you install a new stove?+

Yes. We can discuss the project, survey the installation requirements and provide a quote.

Which areas do you cover?+

We primarily serve Tameside, Mossley, Saddleworth, Oldham, Manchester, Huddersfield and surrounding areas.

How do I get a price?+

Use the booking or quote form and provide a few details. We'll confirm the appropriate service and price.
