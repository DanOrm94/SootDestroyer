How often should I have my chimney swept?+

For many appliances, an annual sweep is a sensible minimum. Frequency can depend on what you burn and how often you use the appliance.

Do I receive a certificate?+

Yes. Chimney sweeping can include a smoke evacuation check and certification where applicable.

Can you install a new log burner?+

Yes. We can advise on your project, discuss suitable stove options and arrange a professional installation.

Which areas do you cover?+

We cover Tameside, Mossley, Saddleworth, Oldham, Manchester, Huddersfield and surrounding areas.
