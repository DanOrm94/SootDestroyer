Why sweep?

It's more than
just removing soot.

Regular chimney sweeping helps remove soot and deposits that can restrict your flue and affect how your appliance performs. It also gives you an opportunity to spot potential issues before they become bigger problems.

Whether you have a log burner, multi-fuel stove or open fire, we'll work carefully and leave the area clean and ready to use.

Chimney sweeping FAQs

Before we
arrive.

Here are a few of the questions homeowners commonly ask.

How often should my chimney be swept?+

Annual sweeping is a sensible guide for many appliances, but the right frequency depends on what you burn and how often you use your fire.

Do I need to prepare the room?+

Please make sure the appliance is cool and accessible. We'll take care of protecting the immediate work area.

Will there be mess?+

We work with cleanliness in mind and aim to leave your room as tidy as we found it.

Can you sweep a log burner?+

Yes. Log burners, multi-fuel stoves and open fires can all be serviced where suitable access is available.
